SKYOM Aura.

Mediterranean Neo-Classical Residences Featuring Arched Colonnades, Streetfront Commercial Suites, and 1500 mm Wide Corridors within the 50-Acre SKYOM City Master Township.

View Floor Plans Download Lookbook PDF ↓

CLASSICAL PROPORTIONS, CONTEMPORARY VIBRANCE

SKYOM Aura brings timeless Mediterranean architecture to Mohanlalganj, Lucknow. Conceived by renowned architects Ar. Nehareeka Mehindru and Ar. Nimit Jain, the development features monumental lower arches, balanced pilasters, private sit-out balconies, and an optional classical balustrade roof terrace.

Situated within the gated sanctuary of SKYOM City, Aura homeowners enjoy instant access to 24+ themed parks, sports zones, wide boulevards, and dedicated doorstep commercial shops designed for modern convenience.
