CLASSICAL PROPORTIONS, CONTEMPORARY VIBRANCE

SKYOM Aura brings timeless Mediterranean architecture to Mohanlalganj, Lucknow. The development features monumental lower arches, balanced pilasters, private sit-out balconies, and an optional classical balustrade roof terrace.

An independent luxury residential project in Mohanlalganj, Lucknow. Aura homeowners enjoy landscaped gardens, modern fitness gym, 24/7 security, wide corridors, and dedicated doorstep commercial shops designed for modern convenience.
