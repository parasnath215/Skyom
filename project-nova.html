SKYOM Nova.

Majestic G+9 Modern Classical High-Rise Tower Featuring Thoughtfully Designed 1 BHK Residences, Dual Elevators, and Full Statutory UP RERA Approval in Lucknow.

Explore Blueprint Download RERA Certificate (PDF) ↓

RISING ELEGANCE IN MOHANLALGANJ

SKYOM Nova represents modern urban engineering at its finest. Standing nine levels tall, this residential tower pairs classical pilasters, decorative cornices, and ambient architectural lighting with contemporary high-rise structural safety standards.

Every 1 BHK residence features generous room dimensions, abundant natural ventilation, private balconies, and dedicated elevator foyers. Promoted by Bihani Constructions Pvt Ltd and Ambalika Construction Ltd, SKYOM Nova is developed under UP RERA Registration No. UPRERAPRJ960284/10/2026.
