RISING ELEGANCE IN MOHANLALGANJ

SKYOM Nova represents modern urban engineering at its finest. Standing nine levels tall, this residential tower pairs classical pilasters, decorative cornices, and ambient architectural lighting with contemporary high-rise structural safety standards.

Every residence features generous room dimensions, abundant natural ventilation, private balconies, and dedicated elevator foyers. Promoted by Bihani Constructions Pvt Ltd and Ambalika Construction Ltd, SKYOM Nova is developed under UP RERA Registration No. UPRERAPRJ960284/10/2026 in Mohanlalganj, Lucknow.
